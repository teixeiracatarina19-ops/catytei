01 / About the Artist

Singer. Songwriter.
Independent Artist.

Caty Tei is a singer, songwriter, and independent artist blending Gospel, Soul, R&B, Pop, and Hip-Hop into a distinctive musical identity shaped by emotion, faith, creativity, and powerful storytelling.

As a songwriter, she explores love, heartbreak, identity, personal struggles, hope, resilience, spirituality, and the experiences that shape human life. Her compositions transform emotions and meaningful stories into music that connects with listeners.

Her artistic vision is inspired by four influential figures in music history: Michael Jackson, for his creativity and innovation; Whitney Houston, for her extraordinary voice and emotional interpretation; CeCe Winans, for her Gospel excellence and spiritual depth; and Tupac Shakur, for his poetic expression, fearless lyricism, and socially conscious storytelling.

Moving between soulful ballads, Gospel-inspired compositions, contemporary R&B, Pop, and Hip-Hop-influenced tracks, Caty Tei aims to create music with authenticity, personality, and purpose.

06 / Broadcasting · Music · News · Sports

Welcome to
Caty Tei Rádio.

A global radio perspective

Every Team. Every Result. One Fair Voice.

Caty Tei Rádio is a bilingual Portuguese-and-English YouTube radio project bringing together music, news, sports, and international entertainment. Its coverage includes football from Portugal and around the world, the NBA, headlines from Portugal and the United States, and music from different cultures.

Built around fairness, respect, and a global perspective, Caty Tei Rádio aims to create an engaging space for audiences everywhere.
